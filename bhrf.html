Our Mission

Preserving India's Heritage, Together.

BHRF aims to work across India's diverse historical heritage — historic temples, mosques, gurudwaras, churches, Buddhist and Jain heritage, monuments, sculptures, architectural elements, inscriptions, traditional artworks and other culturally significant heritage. All are presented equally as parts of one shared national story.
